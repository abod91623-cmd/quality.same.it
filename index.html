Why high bitrate?

TikTok re-compresses every upload. Starting from a high-bitrate source (8–10 Mbps) gives its compressor more detail to preserve, resulting in a cleaner final output.

Why forced 60fps?

Higher framerates reduce macroblocking artifacts introduced during platform re-encoding and produce noticeably smoother motion in-feed.

Privacy

Everything happens locally via WebAssembly. Your video is never uploaded to any server — this page could even work offline once loaded.
